Spiritual & Professional Action Plan

My goal is to continue developing as both a cybersecurity professional and a disciple of Jesus Christ. My spiritual and professional growth are connected because the skills I develop should ultimately allow me to serve others, protect people and their information, and act with integrity.

This action plan builds on the guiding principles that have shaped my portfolio: faith, integrity, service, and continuous learning.

1. Spiritual Growth

Goal: Strengthen my relationship with Jesus Christ and continue becoming a more capable and trusted disciple.

  • Make consistent time for personal prayer and scripture study.
  • Reflect regularly on how gospel principles apply to my decisions and responsibilities.
  • Continue attending the temple and making time for quiet spiritual reflection.
  • Look for opportunities to serve others and strengthen the people around me.

Timeline: Ongoing

I will measure my progress through consistent personal reflection, prayer, scripture study, temple worship, and opportunities to serve. My goal is not simply to complete a checklist, but to develop habits that strengthen my relationship with Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.

2. Professional Cybersecurity Growth

Goal: Continue developing the technical knowledge and practical skills needed to become a capable cybersecurity professional.

  • Continue practicing cybersecurity skills through hands-on labs and projects.
  • Build on my experience with network security, vulnerability assessment, and security testing.
  • Continue learning about emerging cybersecurity threats and defensive technologies.
  • Work toward professional certifications and career opportunities that strengthen my technical abilities.

Timeline: 2026 and ongoing

I will measure professional growth by completing hands-on projects, expanding my technical knowledge, documenting new skills, and progressing toward cybersecurity certifications and employment.

3. Integrity & Ethical Responsibility

Goal: Become a cybersecurity professional who can be trusted with sensitive information and administrative access.

  • Protect confidential information and respect user privacy.
  • Use cybersecurity knowledge only for authorized and ethical purposes.
  • Continue studying real-world security incidents to understand the consequences of poor security decisions.
  • Consider the effect of my technical decisions on people, organizations, and communities.

Timeline: Every professional and academic activity

My ethical standard will be measured by whether I consistently act honestly and responsibly, especially when I have access to sensitive systems or information.

4. Service to Others

Goal: Use my cybersecurity skills as a way to serve and protect others.

  • Look for opportunities to help classmates and coworkers develop technical skills.
  • Use my knowledge to help organizations identify and reduce security risks.
  • Remember that cybersecurity ultimately protects real people, their information, and their livelihoods.
  • Approach professional relationships with patience, respect, and a willingness to help.

Timeline: Ongoing

I will measure this goal through the ways I use my knowledge to help others and by seeking opportunities where my technical abilities can have a positive impact.

5. Continuous Learning

Goal: Develop a lifelong habit of learning both spiritually and professionally.

  • Continue learning new cybersecurity tools, technologies, and defensive techniques.
  • Stay aware of changes in cybersecurity threats and industry practices.
  • Learn from mistakes and feedback instead of being discouraged by them.
  • Seek guidance from Heavenly Father when making important educational and career decisions.

Timeline: Lifelong

Cybersecurity is constantly changing, so I cannot rely only on what I learn in college. I want continuous learning to become part of both my professional identity and my spiritual development.

My Commitment

Moving forward, I want my career to be more than simply a way to earn a living. I want it to be an opportunity to serve. My technical skills, faith, integrity, and commitment to learning should work together to make me someone that employers, coworkers, clients, and the people I serve can trust.

I know that professional growth will require effort and that spiritual growth requires the same kind of intentionality. By continuing to learn, serve, reflect, and rely on Jesus Christ, I can continue becoming the kind of cybersecurity professional and disciple I want to be.
