Portfolio Design Enhancements

Week 5 Usability Testing Report

Usability Testing

I reviewed my portfolio and asked others to evaluate its navigation, readability, organization, and overall appearance. Their feedback helped identify areas where the portfolio could be improved to provide a better user experience.

Design Improvements

  • Improved navigation between portfolio pages.
  • Added contact information to the homepage.
  • Expanded project descriptions.
  • Added technical skills and career goals.
  • Strengthened spiritual reflections.
  • Improved consistency throughout the website.
  • Maintained responsive layouts for different screen sizes.

Impact

These improvements made my portfolio easier to navigate, more professional, and better organized. The updated design clearly demonstrates both my technical skills and my spiritual development while creating a more engaging experience for visitors.
