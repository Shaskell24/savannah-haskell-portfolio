Spiritual Guiding Principles

Faith

Scripture: Doctrine and Covenants 58:27–28

"Men should be anxiously engaged in a good cause, and do many things of their own free will..."

Faith is the foundation of both my spiritual life and my education. This scripture reminds me that God expects me to use my agency to learn, grow, and serve others. Rather than waiting for opportunities, I strive to actively improve my cybersecurity knowledge through coursework, hands-on labs, certifications, and personal projects.

As I continue preparing for a career in cybersecurity, I know that faith means trusting the Lord while putting forth my own effort. I believe He blesses us as we diligently develop our talents and use them to protect and serve others.

General Conference: President Russell M. Nelson, "The Power of Spiritual Momentum"

President Nelson teaches that consistent spiritual growth requires action. His message reminds me that just as cybersecurity requires continual learning and vigilance, my testimony also grows through daily effort and faith.

Integrity

Scripture: Proverbs 3:5–6

"Trust in the Lord with all thine heart..."

Integrity is one of the most important qualities a cybersecurity professional can possess. Security professionals are trusted with sensitive systems, confidential information, and critical infrastructure. I believe that trust must always be earned through honesty, accountability, and ethical decision-making.

Throughout my coursework, I have learned that technical knowledge should always be used responsibly. Whether identifying vulnerabilities or protecting data, I strive to act with honesty and respect for others.

General Conference: Elder Dieter F. Uchtdorf, "Jesus Christ Is the Strength of Youth"

This message reminds me that choosing what is right, even when it is difficult, builds character and strengthens trust. Those same principles guide my future career in cybersecurity.

Service and Lifelong Learning

Scripture: Mosiah 2:17

"When ye are in the service of your fellow beings ye are only in the service of your God."

Technology changes every day, which means cybersecurity professionals must continually learn throughout their careers. I believe that improving my knowledge allows me to better serve individuals and organizations by helping keep their information safe.

During my education at Ensign College, I have developed skills in networking, Linux, AWS, cloud computing, security assessments, and ethical hacking. Every project has strengthened both my technical abilities and my desire to use those skills to serve others.

General Conference: President Thomas S. Monson, "What Have I Done for Someone Today?"

This message reminds me that our talents are meant to bless other people. I hope to use my cybersecurity skills throughout my career to protect organizations, safeguard sensitive information, and help create a safer digital world.

Professional Competencies & Spiritual Growth

Throughout my education at Ensign College, I have learned that technical knowledge alone is not enough to become an effective cybersecurity professional. Skills such as network security, vulnerability assessments, Linux administration, cloud computing, and ethical hacking must be combined with integrity, service, and faith.

My guiding principles influence every decision I make. Faith encourages me to continue learning, integrity reminds me to protect confidential information responsibly, and service motivates me to use my technical abilities to help others. Together, these principles prepare me to become a cybersecurity professional who is technically skilled, ethically responsible, and committed to lifelong growth.
